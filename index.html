⚛︎ STEM Visualizations

Learn science by changing things — not by memorizing facts.

Build intuition, then optimize it.

Every visualization here is interactive and hands-on. You won't just read that carbon has six protons — you'll build the atom, turn it into an ion, make an isotope, share its electrons in a bond, and watch the numbers (charge, mass, energy) respond. The goal is to learn how systems work well enough to change and optimize them.
